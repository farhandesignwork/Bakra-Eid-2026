About Us



Bakra Eid is a special Islamic festival celebrated by Muslims around the world with great joy, love, and devotion. It reminds us of sacrifice, kindness, and helping those in need. On this beautiful occasion, families gather together, offer Qurbani, share delicious meals, and spread happiness among relatives, friends, and the poor. Bakra Eid teaches us the values of unity, gratitude, and caring for others while celebrating the blessings of Allah.
